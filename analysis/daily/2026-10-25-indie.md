**Yes** — low technical complexity. A solo developer could ship an MVP in 1–2 weeks using existing APIs and a simple UI.

### 8. How do I find users?
Post on **r/productivity** and **r/getdisciplined**. Write a 'how I fixed my workflow' blog post that naturally features the tool. Reach out to productivity-focused YouTubers or newsletter writers for an honest review.
